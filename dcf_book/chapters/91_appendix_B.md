# Appendix B — Practice Problems with Solutions {.unnumbered}

Attempt each problem before reading the solution. Where tables of discount factors are needed, compute directly or use Appendix D. Amounts are in ₹ crore unless stated.

## Problems

**1. NPV and IRR.** A project costs ₹800 and returns ₹250, ₹300, ₹350 and ₹400 at the ends of years 1–4. Cost of capital 11%. Compute the NPV and the approximate IRR.

**2. Gordon growth.** A share is expected to pay a dividend of ₹12 next year, growing at 5% for ever. Ke = 13%. (a) Value the share. (b) If the market price is ₹180, what growth rate is the market implying?

**3. FCFF.** EBIT ₹600; tax rate 25%; depreciation ₹150; capex ₹260; operating NWC rises from ₹400 to ₹448. Compute FCFF.

**4. FCFE.** Using the data in Problem 3, net income is ₹380, new debt raised ₹100 and debt repaid ₹40. Compute FCFE.

**5. Beta.** A peer has equity beta 1.15 and D/E 40% (tax 25%). Compute its unlevered beta; re-lever to D/E 30%; compute the resulting cost of equity at Rf 7%, ERP 6%.

**6. WACC.** Equity (market) ₹1,400; debt ₹600; Ke 14%; pre-tax Kd 10%; tax 25%. Compute WACC.

**7. Two-stage DCF.** FCFF for the next five years: 100, 120, 140, 160, 180. WACC 10%; terminal growth 4% (assume FCFF₆ = FCFF₅ × 1.04). Debt 500; cash 80; shares 20 crore. Compute EV, equity value and value per share.

**8. Terminal value consistency.** NOPAT in the first terminal year is ₹250; WACC 10.5%; g = 5%. Compute TV when terminal ROIC is (a) 12.5% and (b) equal to WACC.

**9. EVA.** EBIT ₹420; tax 25%; invested capital ₹2,000; WACC 10.5%. Compute NOPAT, ROIC and EVA.

**10. Mid-year.** Re-value Problem 7 using the mid-year convention (TV by Gordon discounted at 4.5 years). By what percentage does EV change?

**11. APV.** Unlevered FCFF is ₹150 next year, growing 5% for ever. Ku = 12%. The firm will keep ₹800 of debt permanently at 9%; tax rate 25%. Compute APV enterprise value.

**12. Bank.** A bank earns a stable ROE of 16%, has Ke 13% and grows at 6%. Compute the justified P/B. What would P/B be if ROE were 13%?

**13. Venture capital.** A start-up is expected to be worth ₹400 crore in 4 years. An investor wants a 50% IRR and will invest ₹15 crore. Compute post-money, pre-money and the ownership needed at exit; adjust for 20% dilution before exit.

**14. Distress.** Going-concern equity value is ₹800; distress-sale equity value ₹100; probability of distress 35%. Compute the blended equity value.

**15. Currency.** A rupee WACC is 12%; Rf(INR) 7.2%; Rf(USD) 4.0%. Compute the equivalent dollar WACC.

**16. Mini-DCF (three-year explicit period).** A company has revenue of ₹1,000, growing 15%, 12%, 10% in years 1–3; EBIT margins 14%, 15%, 16%; tax 25%; marginal sales-to-capital 2.5. WACC 10%; terminal g 4%; terminal ROIC 12%, terminal margin 16%. Debt ₹300; cash ₹50; 25 crore shares. Compute value per share.

**17. Stub period.** Valuation date 30 September 2026, fiscal year-end 31 March. FY27 FCFF is ₹360 and FY28 FCFF is ₹400. WACC 10%. Using the mid-period convention, compute the PV of (a) the FY27 remaining flow and (b) the FY28 flow.

## Solutions

**1.** NPV at 11%: −800 + 250/1.11 + 300/1.2321 + 350/1.3676 + 400/1.5181 = −800 + 225.23 + 243.49 + 255.92 + 263.49 = **₹188.1**. IRR ≈ **20.7%** (at 20.7% NPV = 0).

**2.** (a) P = 12 ÷ (0.13 − 0.05) = **₹150**. (b) With P = 180: g = Ke − D₁/P = 13% − 12/180 = 13% − 6.67% = **6.33%**.

**3.** NOPAT = 600 × 0.75 = 450; ΔNWC = 48. FCFF = 450 + 150 − 260 − 48 = **₹292**.

**4.** FCFE = 380 + 150 − 260 − 48 + 100 − 40 = **₹282**. (Check: FCFF 292 − after-tax interest + net borrowing 60. After-tax interest implied = 450 − 380 = 70, so 292 − 70 + 60 = 282 ✔.)

**5.** βᵤ = 1.15 ÷ (1 + 0.75 × 0.40) = 1.15 ÷ 1.30 = **0.8846**. Re-levered: 0.8846 × (1 + 0.75 × 0.30) = 0.8846 × 1.225 = **1.0837**. Ke = 7% + 1.0837 × 6% = **13.50%**.

**6.** WACC = (1,400 × 14% + 600 × 10% × 0.75) ÷ 2,000 = (196 + 45) ÷ 2,000 = **12.05%**.

**7.** PVs: 90.91 + 99.17 + 105.18 + 109.28 + 111.77 = **516.31**. TV = 180 × 1.04 ÷ (0.10 − 0.04) = 3,120; PV = 3,120 ÷ 1.61051 = **1,937.27**. EV = **2,453.59**. Equity = 2,453.59 − 500 + 80 = **2,033.59**; per share = **₹101.68**.

**8.** (a) Reinvestment rate = 5% ÷ 12.5% = 40%; FCFF = 250 × 0.60 = 150; TV = 150 ÷ 0.055 = **₹2,727**. (b) ROIC = WACC → TV = NOPAT ÷ WACC = 250 ÷ 0.105 = **₹2,381** (the value of growth at ROIC 12.5% is ₹346).

**9.** NOPAT = 420 × 0.75 = **315**; ROIC = 315 ÷ 2,000 = **15.75%**; EVA = 315 − 0.105 × 2,000 = 315 − 210 = **₹105**.

**10.** PV of explicit flows at t − 0.5: **541.52**; PV of TV at 4.5 years: 3,120 ÷ 1.10^4.5 = **2,031.83**; EV = **2,573.35**, i.e., **+4.9%** (≈ (1.10)^0.5 − 1 = 4.88%).

**11.** Unlevered EV = 150 ÷ (0.12 − 0.05) = **2,142.9**. PV tax shield = 0.25 × 800 = **200**. APV = **₹2,342.9**.

**12.** P/B = (0.16 − 0.06) ÷ (0.13 − 0.06) = 0.10 ÷ 0.07 = **1.43×**. If ROE = Ke = 13%, P/B = (0.13 − 0.06) ÷ (0.13 − 0.06) = **1.00×** — growth adds no value.

**13.** Post-money = 400 ÷ 1.5⁴ = 400 ÷ 5.0625 = **₹79.0 crore**; pre-money = 79.0 − 15 = **₹64.0 crore**; ownership needed at exit = 15 ÷ 79.0 = **19.0%**. With 20% dilution before exit: 19.0% ÷ 0.80 = **23.7%** today.

**14.** 800 × 0.65 + 100 × 0.35 = 520 + 35 = **₹555**.

**15.** Dollar WACC = 1.12 ÷ (1.072/1.040) − 1 = 1.12 ÷ 1.03077 − 1 = **8.66%**.

**16.** Revenue: 1,150; 1,288; 1,416.8. NOPAT: 120.8; 144.9; 170.0. Net reinvestment (ΔRev ÷ 2.5): 60.0; 55.2; 51.5. FCFF: **60.8; 89.7; 118.5**. PV at 10%: 55.3 + 74.1 + 89.0 = **218.4**. Terminal: Revenue₄ = 1,416.8 × 1.04 = 1,473.5; NOPAT₄ = 1,473.5 × 0.16 × 0.75 = 176.8; reinvestment rate = 4% ÷ 12% = 33.3%; FCFF₄ = 117.9; TV₃ = 117.9 ÷ 0.06 = 1,964.6; PV = 1,964.6 ÷ 1.331 = **1,476.1**. EV = **1,694.4**. Equity = 1,694.4 − 300 + 50 = **1,444.4**; per share = **₹57.78**.

**17.** (a) Remaining half-year of FY27 FCFF = 180; mid-point is 0.25 years away: 180 ÷ 1.10^0.25 = **₹175.76**. (b) FY28 cash flows have a mid-point at 0.5 + 0.5 = 1.0 year: 400 ÷ 1.10 = **₹363.64**.
