# Appendix G — Further Practice Problems with Solutions {.unnumbered}

A second problem set, slightly more demanding than Appendix B. Amounts in ₹ crore unless stated.

## Problems

**G1. Growth identity.** A firm earns ROIC of 16% on new investment and wants to grow NOPAT at 8% a year. What reinvestment rate is required? If NOPAT is ₹400, what is FCFF?

**G2. Equity bridge.** Enterprise value ₹5,000. Borrowings 1,200; lease liabilities 300; surplus cash 450; non-controlling interests (market-based) 400; a 30% associate with an equity value to the group of 250; an unfunded pension deficit of ₹80 pre-tax (tax 25%). Shares 50 crore. Compute equity value and value per share.

**G3. Terminal-value share.** PV of explicit cash flows is ₹800; terminal value at year 5 is ₹4,200; WACC 10%. Compute EV and the terminal value's share of EV.

**G4. Beta and cash.** The unlevered beta of a company *including* its cash is 0.80. Cash is 20% of firm value (cash beta ≈ 0). What is the beta of the operating business?

**G5. Stable P/E.** A stable firm has payout ratio 60%, growth 6% and cost of equity 12.5%. Compute the justified (forward-adjusted) P/E: P₀/EPS₀.

**G6. EVA and MVA.** NOPAT ₹270; invested capital ₹1,800; WACC 11%; market value of the firm ₹2,500. Compute EVA and MVA.

**G7. Implied growth from an exit multiple.** A buyer assumes an exit value of ₹4,500 at year 5 (9× EBITDA of 500). Year-6 FCFF would be ₹260 and WACC is 11%. What perpetual growth rate does the exit value imply?

**G8. Options in the equity value.** Equity value *before* option exercise is ₹1,000; 10 crore basic shares; 2 crore options with exercise price ₹40. Compute the value per share (options in the money).

**G9. Lease consistency.** Before Ind AS 116: EBITDA after rent ₹400; EV (excluding leases) ₹4,000. After Ind AS 116: rent of ₹60 is excluded from EBITDA and a lease liability of ₹450 is recognised. Compute EV/EBITDA on both bases and comment.

**G10. Control and marketability.** A minority, marketable stake is worth ₹100. Control premium 30%; DLOM for an unlisted minority stake 20%. Compute (a) the control value, (b) the DLOC implied relative to control value, (c) the value of an unlisted minority stake.

**G11. Declining tax shield.** A firm repays ₹1,000 of debt in four equal annual instalments (₹250 a year), interest at 10% on the opening balance, tax 25%, tax shield discounted at 10%. Compute the PV of the tax shield.

**G12. Four-year mid-year DCF.** Revenue ₹2,000 growing 10%, 10%, 8%, 8%; EBIT margin 15% throughout; tax 25%; marginal sales-to-capital 2.0; WACC 11%; terminal growth 5% and terminal ROIC 15% (margin 15%). Debt ₹800; cash ₹100; 40 crore shares. Mid-year discounting (TV discounted at 3.5 years). Compute the value per share.

**G13. Scenario weighting.** Bear, base and bull values are ₹60, ₹100 and ₹150 per share with probabilities 25%, 50% and 25%. Compute the expected value. What is the standard deviation?

**G14. WACC.** Ke 14%; pre-tax Kd 9%; tax 25%; D/V 30%. Compute WACC. If the debt ratio rises to 40% and Ke rises to 15.5% with Kd of 9.75%, compute the new WACC and comment.

## Solutions

**G1.** Reinvestment rate = g/ROIC = 8% ÷ 16% = **50%**. FCFF = 400 × (1 − 0.50) = **₹200**.

**G2.** Equity = 5,000 − 1,200 − 300 + 450 − 400 + 250 − 60 (pension 80 × 0.75) = **₹3,740**. Per share = 3,740 ÷ 50 = **₹74.80**.

**G3.** PV of TV = 4,200 ÷ 1.10⁵ = 2,607.9; EV = 800 + 2,607.9 = **₹3,407.9**; TV share = 2,607.9 ÷ 3,407.9 = **76.5%**.

**G4.** β_operating = 0.80 ÷ (1 − 0.20) = **1.00**. (Cash dilutes the observed beta; the operating assets are riskier than the diluted figure suggests.)

**G5.** P/E = payout × (1 + g) ÷ (Ke − g) = 0.60 × 1.06 ÷ 0.065 = **9.78×**.

**G6.** EVA = 270 − 0.11 × 1,800 = 270 − 198 = **₹72**. MVA = 2,500 − 1,800 = **₹700**.

**G7.** 4,500 = 260 ÷ (0.11 − g) → 0.11 − g = 0.05778 → g = **5.22%**. This is a plausible but not undemanding rate; compare it with the risk-free rate and the economy's long-run growth.

**G8.** Exercise proceeds = 2 × 40 = 80. Equity after exercise = 1,000 + 80 = 1,080 over 12 crore shares: **₹90 per share**. (Check by treasury-stock method: 2 × (1 − 40/90) = 1.11 net new shares; 1,000 ÷ 11.11 = 90 ✔.)

**G9.** Before: EV/EBITDA = 4,000 ÷ 400 = **10.0×**. After: EBITDA = 460; EV including leases = 4,000 + 450 = 4,450; EV/EBITDA = 4,450 ÷ 460 = **9.67×**. The multiple drops slightly because the lease-adjusted EV rises by less (11.25%) than EBITDA (15%). *Never* divide an EV that excludes leases by an EBITDA that is before rent (it would be 4,000 ÷ 460 = 8.7×) — that mixes bases.

**G10.** (a) Control value = 100 × 1.30 = **₹130**. (b) DLOC = 1 − 1/1.30 = **23.1%**. (c) Unlisted minority stake = 100 × (1 − 0.20) = **₹80**.

**G11.** Interest: 100, 75, 50, 25; tax shields: 25.0, 18.75, 12.5, 6.25; PV at 10%: 22.73 + 15.50 + 9.39 + 4.27 = **₹51.9**.

**G12.** Revenue: 2,200; 2,420; 2,613.6; 2,822.7. NOPAT (15% × 0.75 of revenue): 247.5; 272.2; 294.0; 317.6. Net reinvestment (ΔRevenue ÷ 2.0): 100.0; 110.0; 96.8; 104.5. FCFF: **147.5; 162.2; 197.2; 213.0**. PV at 0.5, 1.5, 2.5, 3.5 years = **578.5**. Terminal: Revenue₅ = 2,822.7 × 1.05 = 2,963.8; NOPAT₅ = 333.4; reinvestment rate = 5% ÷ 15% = 33.3%; FCFF₅ = 222.3; TV = 222.3 ÷ 0.06 = 3,704.8; PV = 3,704.8 ÷ 1.11^3.5 = **2,571.2**. EV = **3,149.7**. Equity = 3,149.7 − 800 + 100 = **2,449.7**; per share = **₹61.24**.

**G13.** Expected value = 0.25 × 60 + 0.50 × 100 + 0.25 × 150 = **₹102.5**. Variance = 0.25 × (60 − 102.5)² + 0.50 × (100 − 102.5)² + 0.25 × (150 − 102.5)² = 0.25 × 1,806.25 + 0.5 × 6.25 + 0.25 × 2,256.25 = 451.56 + 3.13 + 564.06 = 1,018.75; s.d. = **₹31.9**.

**G14.** First WACC = 0.70 × 14% + 0.30 × 9% × 0.75 = 9.80% + 2.025% = **11.83%**. Second: 0.60 × 15.5% + 0.40 × 9.75% × 0.75 = 9.30% + 2.925% = **12.23%**. WACC *rises* because Ke and Kd both rise with leverage by more than the weight shift lowers the blend; adding "cheap" debt does not automatically reduce WACC once the cost of equity is re-levered (Chapter 11).
