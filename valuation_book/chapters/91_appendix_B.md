# Appendix B — Glossary {.unnumbered}

**Adjusted present value (APV).** Value of the unlevered firm plus tax benefits of debt minus expected bankruptcy costs.

**Arm's length.** A transaction between independent parties acting in their own interest.

**Arbitrage Pricing Theory (APT).** A multi-factor model of expected return: Rf plus the sum of factor betas times factor premia.

**Asset beta (ungeared beta).** Beta of a business's assets, reflecting business risk only.

**Beta.** Measure of a security's sensitivity to market movements (systematic risk).

**Berkus approach.** A start-up valuation method assigning value to five success factors, capped at $2 million pre-revenue and $2.5 million post-revenue.

**Capitalisation rate.** The rate at which maintainable earnings are converted into value; approximately EPS/price or 1/PE.

**CAPM.** Capital Asset Pricing Model: R = Rf + β(Rm − Rf).

**Chop-shop method.** Valuation of a multi-industry company by valuing its segments separately using pure-play capitalisation ratios and averaging.

**Company-specific risk premium (CSRP).** An add-on to the cost of equity for risks not captured by beta (alpha).

**Cost-to-duplicate approach.** Start-up value as the cost of replicating the business, including development and assets.

**De-gearing / re-gearing.** Removing / re-applying the effect of financial leverage on beta.

**Digital platform.** Software-based online infrastructure that facilitates many-to-many interactions.

**Distress sale value.** Amount realised if a distressed firm's assets are sold in stress.

**DCF.** Discounted cash flow valuation.

**EBITDA.** Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation.

**Enterprise value (EV).** Market capitalisation plus debt minus cash.

**ESG.** Environmental, Social and Governance factors.

**EVA.** Economic Value Added: NOPAT minus capital charge.

**Fair value.** Price in an orderly transaction between market participants; for an acquirer, the price "no less, no more".

**First Chicago method.** A start-up valuation combining DCF and market approaches across worst, normal and best scenarios with probabilities.

**Free cash flow.** Cash available to providers of capital after operating costs, taxes and reinvestment.

**Going concern.** Assumption that an enterprise will continue operating for the foreseeable future.

**Gordon model.** Valuation assuming dividends grow at a constant rate for ever: P₀ = D₁/(Ke − g).

**Goodwill.** Value of a business above the value of its identifiable net assets.

**Horizon value.** Another name for terminal value.

**Invested capital.** Total assets less non-interest-bearing current liabilities (equity plus long-term debt).

**IRR.** Internal rate of return: the discount rate at which NPV is zero.

**Leveraged buy-out (LBO).** Acquisition financed mainly with debt (typically ~70:30 debt to equity), repaid from the target's cash flows.

**Liquidation value.** Net value realised after disposing of all assets and discharging liabilities.

**Modified DCF.** DCF using probability-weighted cash flows that reflect the likelihood of distress.

**MVA.** Market Value Added: market value of the firm minus invested capital.

**Net asset value (NAV).** Assets minus liabilities at book value.

**Net realisable value (NRV).** Realisable value of assets less liquidation expenses and liabilities.

**NOPAT.** Net operating profit after tax: EBIT(1 − t).

**Non-going-concern valuation.** Liquidation valuation.

**Normalisation.** Adjusting reported figures to maintainable levels.

**P/E ratio.** Price per share divided by earnings per share.

**PEG ratio.** P/E divided by the expected growth rate.

**Present value.** Value today of a future cash flow, discounted at a rate reflecting time and risk.

**Pure-play firm.** A company engaged entirely in one business, used as a proxy for beta or multiples.

**Relative valuation.** Valuation by comparison with peers using multiples.

**Replaceable value.** Cost of acquiring equivalent assets in the open market.

**ROI.** Return on investment.

**Scorecard method.** Pre-revenue start-up valuation adjusting the average valuation of comparables by weighted factors.

**SVA.** Shareholder Value Analysis: DCF organised around value drivers.

**Terminal value.** Value of the business at the end of the explicit forecast period.

**Value drivers.** Factors that determine value (sales growth, margin, investment, cost of capital, forecast period).

**Venture capital method.** Start-up valuation by discounting expected exit value at the investor's required multiple or IRR.

**WACC.** Weighted average cost of capital.
