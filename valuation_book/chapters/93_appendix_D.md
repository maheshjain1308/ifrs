# Appendix D — A Second Problem Set (Exam-Style) with Solutions {.unnumbered}

Fifteen further problems, slightly longer than those of Chapter 22, in the manner of the syllabus questions. Amounts in ₹ crore unless stated. Attempt each before reading the solution.

## Problems

**S1 — Asset values.** Fixed assets ₹1,200; net current assets ₹500; long-term debt ₹700; 20 crore shares. Realisable values: fixed assets ₹1,100; net current assets ₹430; liquidation expenses ₹40. Replacement cost of fixed assets ₹1,400; net current assets at book. Compute value per share on book, net realisable and replaceable bases.

**S2 — Earnings approach with adjustments.** Reported profit after tax ₹90 crore (tax 30%) includes extraordinary income of ₹14 crore and an extraordinary loss of ₹6 crore (both pre-tax). A new product will add ₹25 crore of profit before tax. Preference dividend ₹12 crore; 10 crore equity shares; capitalisation rate 15%; P/E for equity 9. Compute (a) the value of the business; (b) the market price per equity share.

**S3 — Two-stage FCFF.** Base year: revenue ₹1,000; EBIT ₹160; capex ₹120; depreciation ₹80. For 3 years revenue, EBIT, capex and depreciation grow 15%; thereafter 6% with capex equal to depreciation. Working capital is 20% of revenue; tax 30%. WACC 12% in the high-growth years and 10% in the stable phase. Compute the value of the firm.

**S4 — Beta, WACC and value.** Asset beta 0.8; target D:E = 30:70; tax 25%; risk-free 6%; market return 12.5%; pre-tax cost of debt 11%. EBITDA ₹120 includes an extraordinary gain of ₹8 and a pending write-off of ₹12; EV multiple 6. Future free cash flows: ₹50, ₹60, ₹75. Compute βₑ, Ke, WACC, adjusted EBITDA, EV by multiple and the PV of the three flows.

**S5 — EVA.** Revenue ₹2,000; direct costs ₹900; SG&A ₹400 (including a bad-debt provision of ₹30); interest ₹40; tax 30%. Total assets ₹3,000; non-interest-bearing current liabilities ₹700; debt ₹600; equity ₹1,700 (the same provision reduces receivables). Ke 14%; pre-tax Kd 10%. Compute EVA.

**S6 — Exchange ratio with three methods.** Company A: NAV per share ₹150; earnings value ₹300; market price ₹360. Company B: ₹60; ₹120; ₹150. Weights 1 : 2 : 3. Compute the exchange ratio.

**S7 — Chop-shop by EV/EBITDA.** Segment EBITDAs: A ₹200 (peer multiple 7×); B ₹150 (9×); C ₹90 (11×). Unallocated corporate costs ₹30 capitalised at 8×. Net debt ₹600; 25 crore shares. Compute value per share.

**S8 — Distressed company (modified DCF).** Cash flows if surviving: ₹80, ₹100, ₹130 plus a terminal value of ₹900 at year 3. Cumulative probabilities of distress by year 1, 2, 3: 10%, 25%, 40%. Discount rate 13%. Compute the expected value and compare with the value if survival were certain.

**S9 — Scorecard.** Average pre-money valuation of comparable start-ups ₹20 crore. Comparison percentages: team 120% (weight 30%), opportunity 100% (25%), product 110% (15%), competition 90% (10%), marketing 80% (10%), funding need 100% (5%), others 100% (5%). Compute the pre-money value.

**S10 — LBO.** Buy a business with EBITDA ₹100 at 6×; 70% debt. EBITDA grows 10% a year for 4 years; debt repaid ₹200 over the period; exit at 6×. Compute sponsor equity, exit equity, money multiple and IRR.

**S11 — Digital platform.** A comparable platform was acquired at ₹350 per active user. The target has 15 lakh active users and deserves a 10% premium for faster growth. Value it.

**S12 — ESG.** FCFF next year ₹150; WACC 11%; growth 4%. (a) A social-compliance cost of ₹10 pre-tax a year (tax 30%, growing 4%) will arise. (b) A governance penalty of ₹6 pre-tax is expected in each of the next two years. Compute the effect on EV of each.

**S13 — Enterprise value.** 5 crore shares at ₹90; debt ₹150; cash ₹60; EBITDA ₹70. Compute market capitalisation, EV and EV/EBITDA.

**S14 — Going versus liquidation.** DCF value ₹700. Assets could be sold for ₹920, selling costs ₹40, tax on gains ₹30; liabilities settled separately (ignore). What is the better course and what is the going-concern premium or discount?

**S15 — SVA.** FCFs: ₹40, ₹45, ₹50, then ₹50 a year for ever from year 4; WACC 12%; non-core investments ₹30; debt ₹60. Compute the equity value (exact discounting of the perpetuity).

## Solutions

**S1.** *Book:* 1,200 + 500 − 700 = **₹1,000** → **₹50** per share. *NRV:* 1,100 + 430 − 40 − 700 = **₹790** → **₹39.5**. *Replaceable:* 1,400 + 500 − 700 = **₹1,200** → **₹60**. Here NRV is *below* book because debtors/stock are written down and costs deducted; replacement is the highest.

**S2.** (a) PBT = 90 ÷ 0.7 = 128.57; remove the extraordinary income (−14) and the extraordinary loss (+6): 120.57; add new product 25: **145.57**; tax 30%: PAT = **101.9**; value = 101.9 ÷ 0.15 = **₹679.3 crore**. (b) Earnings for equity = 101.9 − 12 = **89.9**; EPS = 8.99; price = 8.99 × 9 = **₹80.9**.

**S3.** *High growth:* FCFF = EBIT × 0.7 − (capex − depreciation) − 20% × Δrevenue.
Year 1: 184 × 0.7 = 128.8 − (138 − 92 = 46) − 0.2 × 150 = 30 → **52.8**. Year 2: 211.6 × 0.7 = 148.1 − 52.9 − 34.5 = **60.72**. Year 3: 243.3 × 0.7 = 170.3 − 60.8 − 39.7 = **69.83**.
PV at 12% = 47.14 + 48.40 + 49.70 = **145.25**.
*Terminal year (growth 6%):* EBIT₄ = 243.34 × 1.06 = 257.94; EAT = 180.56; net capex 0; revenue₃ = 1,520.9 and revenue₄ = 1,520.9 × 1.06 = 1,612.2, so Δrevenue = 91.3 and ΔNWC = 18.26; FCFF₄ = 180.56 − 18.26 = **162.31**. TV = 162.31 ÷ (0.10 − 0.06) = **4,057.7**; PV (÷ 1.12³) = **2,888.2**.
**Value of the firm = 145.25 + 2,888.19 = ₹3,033.4 crore** (terminal value 95%).

**S4.** βₑ = 0.8 × [1 + 0.75 × 30/70] = 0.8 × 1.3214 = **1.057**; Ke = 6% + 1.057 × 6.5% = **12.87%**; WACC = 12.87% × 0.70 + 11% × 0.75 × 0.30 = 9.01% + 2.475% = **11.485%**. Adjusted EBITDA = 120 − 8 − 12 = **100**; EV = 6 × 100 = **600**. Discount factors 0.897, 0.805, 0.722 → PVs 44.85 + 48.27 + 54.13 = **147.25** (three-year flows only; no terminal value).

**S5.** NOPAT = (2,000 − 900 − 400) × 0.7 + 30 = 490 + 30 = **520**. Invested capital = 3,000 − 700 + 30 = **2,330**. WACC = (1,700 × 14% + 600 × 10% × 0.7) ÷ 2,300 = (238 + 42) ÷ 2,300 = **12.17%**. Capital charge = 283.65. **EVA = 520 − 283.65 = ₹236.35 crore.**

**S6.** A = (150×1 + 300×2 + 360×3) ÷ 6 = (150 + 600 + 1,080) ÷ 6 = **₹305**. B = (60 + 240 + 450) ÷ 6 = **₹125**. Ratio = 125 ÷ 305 = **0.4098** A shares per B share.

**S7.** A: 200 × 7 = 1,400; B: 150 × 9 = 1,350; C: 90 × 11 = 990; total 3,740; less capitalised corporate costs 30 × 8 = 240 → **EV 3,500**. Equity = 3,500 − 600 = **2,900**; per share = **₹116**.

**S8.** Expected cash flows: Year 1: 80 × 0.90 = 72; Year 2: 100 × 0.75 = 75; Year 3: (130 + 900) × 0.60 = 618. PV at 13%: 63.7 + 58.7 + 428.3 = **₹550.8**. Survival certain: 70.8 + 78.3 + 714.0 = **₹863.0**. Distress probabilities remove 36% of value.

**S9.** Factors: 0.36 + 0.25 + 0.165 + 0.09 + 0.08 + 0.05 + 0.05 = **1.045**; value = 1.045 × 20 = **₹20.9 crore**.

**S10.** Entry EV = 600; debt = 420; equity = **180**. EBITDA in year 4 = 100 × 1.1⁴ = 146.4; exit EV = 878.5; debt at exit = 420 − 200 = 220; **exit equity = 658.5**. Multiple = 3.66×; IRR = 3.66^(1/4) − 1 = **38.3%**. Source of return: EBITDA growth (46.4 × 6 = 278), debt paydown (200) and no multiple expansion; equity gain = 478.5 = 278.5 + 200 ✔.

**S11.** 15 lakh × ₹350 = ₹52.5 crore; with a 10% premium: **₹57.75 crore**.

**S12.** Baseline EV = 150 ÷ 0.07 = **₹2,142.9**. (a) After-tax social cost = 10 × 0.7 = 7; EV = (150 − 7) ÷ 0.07 = **₹2,042.9** → −₹100 (**−4.7%**). (b) Penalty after tax = 4.2 in each of two years: PV = 4.2 ÷ 1.11 + 4.2 ÷ 1.2321 = 3.78 + 3.41 = **₹7.2 crore** (−0.3%) — material to the board but small in value.

**S13.** Market cap = 5 crore × ₹90 = **₹450 crore**; EV = 450 + 150 − 60 = **₹540 crore**; EV/EBITDA = 540 ÷ 70 = **7.7×**.

**S14.** Net liquidation proceeds = 920 − 40 − 30 = **₹850** > going-concern value 700. The business is worth **₹150 crore more liquidated** (going-concern *discount* of 150, or 17.6% below liquidation value). Rational course: sell/liquidate (or restructure to raise going-concern value above 850). Valuation on a going-concern basis is justified only if value exceeds liquidation value.

**S15.** PV of years 1–3: 40 ÷ 1.12 + 45 ÷ 1.2544 + 50 ÷ 1.4049 = 35.71 + 35.87 + 35.59 = **107.18**. Perpetuity of 50 from year 4: value at the end of year 3 = 50 ÷ 0.12 = 416.67; PV = 416.67 ÷ 1.4049 = **296.58**. Total PV = 403.75; **equity = 403.75 + 30 − 60 = ₹373.75 crore.**
