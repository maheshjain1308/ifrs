How to find the current text

  1. Search the SEBI website’s legal framework section for the instrument by name and use the latest consolidated version.
  2. Check master circulars on public issues, debt, REITs/InvITs and listing obligations.
  3. Check BSE and NSE circulars for bidding and listing procedure.
  4. Check the Ministry of Corporate Affairs for Companies Act rules and amendments.
  5. Confirm with counsel; ask whether any amendment is pending or recently notified.
