These checklists condense the book into action lists. They are starting points: tailor them to the issuer, and verify every item against current regulations (Chapter 29).

1. Eligibility and structure

  • No SEBI debarment of issuer, directors, promoters, promoter group or selling shareholders
  • No wilful defaulter or fugitive economic offender among directors or promoters
  • No director or promoter linked to a debarred company
  • Track-record tests (NTA ≥ ₹3 crore, average operating profit ≥ ₹15 crore, net worth ≥ ₹1 crore, each over three full years) — or institutional route chosen with ≥ 75% to QIBs
  • Monetary assets ≤ 50% of NTA (or commitments made, or pure OFS)
  • Name-change revenue test satisfied if the name changed within the last year
  • No outstanding convertibles or option rights (except ESOPs and convertibles to be converted before RHP)
  • Minimum offer to public computed for the expected post-IPO market value
  • Offer structure fixed: fresh issue / OFS / combination; pre-IPO placement considered

2. Promoters and capital structure

  • Promoters, promoter group and group companies identified and documented
  • All promoter shares dematerialised
  • Promoters’ contribution (20% of post-IPO fully diluted capital) computed using eligible shares only
  • Pledged, recently acquired, non-cash and bonus shares from revaluation reserves excluded from the contribution
  • Lock-in schedule drawn for promoters, pre-IPO holders, VC/AIF/FVCI, ESOP holders and anchors
  • Selling shareholders meet the one-year holding rule or an exception

3. Governance

  • Board composition meets non-executive and independent-director proportions for the type of chairman
  • At least one woman director
  • Audit, nomination and remuneration, stakeholders’ relationship, risk management (if applicable) and CSR committees constituted
  • Independent director of the issuer on the board of each material subsidiary
  • Policies adopted: related-party transactions, materiality, whistleblower, insider trading codes, dividend and others as required
  • Memorandum and articles amended; special shareholder rights terminated
  • Peer-reviewed statutory auditor appointed

4. Approvals and appointments

  • Board resolution for the issue and the offer documents
  • Special resolution of shareholders for a fresh issue
  • Selling shareholders’ own corporate authorisations
  • Lender, JV-partner and shareholder-agreement consents
  • RBI / sector-regulator approvals where required
  • BRLMs, legal counsel, registrar, banks, advertising agency and (if applicable) monitoring agency appointed
  • Engagement letters signed

5. Offer document and documents

  • Audited, restated and consolidated financials for three fiscals plus stub period; not more than six months old
  • Pro forma statements for material acquisitions or divestments (20% test)
  • Statement of special tax benefits
  • Objects of the issue supported by lender details, auditor certificate (for loan repayment) and working-capital assessment
  • Litigation disclosures per board materiality policy; five-year disciplinary-action history of promoters
  • Directors’ and KMP’s disclosures
  • Government approvals mapped
  • Issue agreement, registrar agreement, service provider agreement executed before DRHP
  • Standard certificates from issuer, directors, promoters, group companies and sellers; comfort letters
  • DRHP filed through BRLM with due diligence certificate; in-principle applications to exchanges

6. SEBI review to RHP

  • Interim observations answered; DRHP updated
  • Final observations received (valid for 12 months)
  • Cash escrow, share escrow (OFS) and syndicate agreements executed
  • Updated certificates and comfort letter delivered
  • RHP registered with RoC at least three days before issue opening
  • Pre-issue advertisement (English, Hindi and regional newspapers)

7. Offer period

  • Price band announced at least two working days before opening
  • Anchor bidding completed one day before opening; CANs issued
  • Bidding open for at least three working days; ASBA/UPI processes tested
  • Bids reconciled with exchange data by the Registrar
  • Issue Price fixed; underwriting agreement signed; Prospectus registered with the RoC

8. Post-issue

  • Basis of allotment approved by the designated exchange
  • Funds transferred to the Public Issue Account; refunds / unblocking done
  • Allotment within four working days of closing; demat credit within two working days (2019 framework)
  • Listing and trading application filed; final approvals received
  • Post-issue advertisement within ten days (Regulation 51)
  • Monitoring agency reporting and insider-trading codes in place

9. Quick checklists for other routes

QIP

  • Special resolution with relevant date; listed ≥ 1 year; six-month gap since last QIP
  • No promoter or promoter-related allottees; at least 10% to mutual funds; minimum allottees; no QIB above 50%
  • Price ≥ two-week average of weekly high and low closing prices (discount ≤ 5% if approved)
  • Prior intimation to exchanges of board meeting

Rights issue

  • Record date, ratio and price decided
  • Fast-track conditions (13) tested; otherwise draft letter of offer to SEBI
  • Limited-disclosure conditions tested
  • Promoters’ intention to subscribe confirmed

Preferential issue

  • Special resolution naming the class of allottees; allottees’ demat holdings and six-month sale history checked
  • Price floor calculated at the relevant date (30 days before resolution)
  • Allotment within 15 days; shares fully paid; lock-in applied

NCD listing

  • Credit rating, debenture trustee, depositories, RTA appointed
  • Borrowing-limit and security approvals (Sections 180(1)(c), 180(1)(a))
  • Disclosure document / prospectus; in-principle approval; debt listing agreement
  • Return of allotment within 15 days (private); trust deed within 60 days
