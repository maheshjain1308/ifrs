Terms are listed alphabetically. Defined terms in the SEBI Regulations (such as Bid, Issue Period and Price Band) have the meanings below as used in the handbook. Rupee thresholds are given in rupees with crore equivalents.

TermMeaning
AIFAlternative Investment Fund registered with SEBI under the SEBI AIF Regulations, 2012.
AllotmentIssue or transfer of equity shares to successful bidders pursuant to the issue.
Anchor InvestorA QIB that bids for ₹10 crore (₹100 million) or more in a book-built IPO before the issue opens to the public; allocation is discretionary within limits (Chapter 10).
ASBAApplication Supported by Blocked Amount: an application containing an authorisation to block the application money in the investor’s bank account until allotment.
ASBA BidderAn investor who applies through the ASBA process.
Banker to an IssueA bank registered under the SEBI (Bankers to an Issue) Regulations, 1994 that handles issue money (escrow, refund, public issue account).
Basis of AllotmentThe pattern in which securities are allotted among different categories of applicants, finalised by the registrar with the designated exchange.
BidAn indication to make an offer, during the issue period, to subscribe to shares at a price within the price band, including all revisions.
Bid AmountThe highest value of the optional bids indicated in the Bid cum Application Form and payable on submission.
Bid cum Application FormThe form by which a bidder offers to subscribe to shares; it serves as the application for allotment.
BidderAny prospective investor who makes a bid under the Red Herring Prospectus.
Book Building ProcessA process by which demand for securities is elicited and built up and the price is assessed to determine the quantum of securities to be issued.
BRLMBook Running Lead Manager: SEBI-registered merchant banker responsible for due diligence and other IPO activities.
BSEBSE Limited, a recognised stock exchange.
CANConfirmation of Allocation Note: advice of allocation sent to anchor investors on finalisation of the allocation.
Cap PriceThe upper end of the price band, above which the Issue Price will not be fixed and no bids accepted.
CDPCollecting Depository Participant: a depository participant eligible to collect bids at designated locations.
Comfort letterA letter from the auditors to BRLMs giving negative assurance on changes in line items after the date of the last audit and confirming financial data in the offer document.
Companies ActCompanies Act, 2013 and the rules made under it.
Corporate GovernanceThe way in which companies run themselves and are accountable to those with a vested interest in their performance, especially shareholders.
Debenture RulesCompanies (Share Capital and Debenture) Rules, 2014.
Debenture TrusteeA SEBI-registered trustee that acts for and on behalf of NCD holders.
DematerialisationThe conversion of securities held in physical form into electronic form through a depository participant.
Depositories ActDepositories Act, 1996.
DepositoryAn organisation that holds securities in electronic form for its depositors; in India, NSDL and CDSL.
Depository ParticipantAn agent of a depository through which the investor interfaces with the depository.
Designated DateThe date on which funds are transferred from the Escrow Account to the Public Issue Account after the Prospectus is filed with the RoC; the board then allots the shares.
DRHPDraft Red Herring Prospectus: draft offer document filed with SEBI that does not contain complete particulars of the issue price.
Due diligence certificateA certificate from BRLMs to SEBI confirming that they have exercised due diligence on the offer document.
Electronic Book ProviderA stock-exchange platform for secure electronic bidding, used for NCD issues of ₹200 crore or more.
Equity SharesShares of the issuer carrying residual ownership and voting rights.
Escrow AccountAn account with an Escrow Collection Bank into which anchor investors pay their bid amounts.
Escrow AgreementAgreement for collection of bid amounts and refund of amounts collected (excluding ASBA bidders).
Escrow Collection BankThe bank at which the Escrow Account is opened.
FEMAForeign Exchange Management Act, 1999.
FiscalTwelve months ending 31 March.
Floor PriceThe lower end of the price band, below which the Issue Price will not be fixed and no bids accepted.
FPIForeign Portfolio Investor registered under the SEBI FPI Regulations.
FPOFurther Public Offer: a public offer by a listed company.
Fugitive Economic OffenderAn individual declared to be a fugitive economic offender under Section 12 of the Fugitive Economic Offenders Act, 2018.
FVCIForeign Venture Capital Investor registered with SEBI.
Green Shoe OptionAn option to over-allot up to 15% of the issue size and operate a post-listing price stabilisation mechanism through a stabilising agent.
Group companiesCompanies (other than promoters and subsidiaries) with related-party transactions in the disclosure period, plus others the board considers material.
Ind-ASIndian Accounting Standards.
Insider tradingDealing in securities by persons holding unpublished price-sensitive information.
Institutional InvestorsOrganisations that invest, such as insurance companies, depository institutions, pension funds, investment companies and endowments.
InvITInfrastructure Investment Trust.
IPOInitial Public Offering.
Issue Closing DateThe date after which syndicate members shall not accept any bids.
Issue Opening DateThe date on which syndicate members begin accepting bids.
Issue PeriodThe period between the Issue Opening Date and the Issue Closing Date, inclusive.
Issue PriceThe price at which allotment is made, determined by the issuer in consultation with BRLMs on the Pricing Date.
LLPLimited Liability Partnership.
Lock-inA restriction on selling, transferring or pledging shares for a specified period.
Merchant BankerA person engaged in issue management — arranging, selling, buying or subscribing to securities or acting as manager, consultant or adviser.
Monitoring AgencyA public financial institution or scheduled commercial bank that monitors the use of IPO proceeds where the fresh issue exceeds ₹100 crore.
NCDsNon-convertible debentures.
NIINon-Institutional Investor: any bidder that is neither a QIB nor a Retail Individual Investor.
NSENational Stock Exchange of India Limited.
Offer For SaleAn offer of securities by existing shareholders to the public through an offer document.
PAS RulesCompanies (Prospectus and Allotment of Securities) Rules, 2014.
Pre-IPO placementIssue of shares or convertibles after filing the DRHP and before filing the RHP.
Preferential allotmentIssue of shares to a select group of persons in preference to all existing shareholders.
Price BandThe range between the Floor Price and Cap Price (both inclusive), including revisions.
Pricing DateThe date on which the Issue Price is finalised.
PromoterA person identified as promoter in the offer document or annual return, who controls the issuer, or on whose advice the board is accustomed to act.
Promoter groupPersons and entities related to the promoter by family or by 20% ownership links, as defined in the SEBI Regulations.
ProspectusThe final offer document filed with the RoC containing the Issue Price, issue size and other information.
Public Issue AccountAccount opened with the Bankers to the Issue to receive money from the Escrow Account on the Designated Date.
QIBQualified Institutional Buyer: public financial institutions, FPIs, scheduled commercial banks, mutual funds, VCFs, AIFs, FVCIs, state industrial development corporations, insurers, provident and pension funds with corpus of at least ₹25 crore, multilateral and bilateral development financial institutions, the National Investment Fund and certain government insurance funds.
QIB PortionThe portion of the issue available for allocation to QIBs.
QIPQualified Institutions Placement.
Red Herring ProspectusOffer document without complete particulars of price and size; filed with the RoC at least three days before the issue opens and becomes the Prospectus after pricing.
Registered BrokersStock brokers registered with exchanges having nationwide terminals, other than syndicate members.
Registrar of CompaniesThe registrar with whom the issuer is registered.
Registrar to the IssueSEBI-registered registrar that processes applications and coordinates allotment and refunds.
REITReal Estate Investment Trust.
RestatementRe-presentation of historical financial statements to ensure consistency of accounting policies and presentation.
Retail Individual InvestorIndividual bidder (including HUFs and NRIs) bidding for up to ₹2 lakh in any option.
Rights IssueAn issue of new securities to existing shareholders in a fixed ratio.
RTARegistrar and Share Transfer Agent.
SCRA / SCRRSecurities Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1956 / Rules, 1957.
SCSBSelf-Certified Syndicate Bank: a banker to an issue offering the ASBA service.
SEBISecurities and Exchange Board of India.
SEBI Regulations (ICDR)SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018.
Sponsor BankA banker to the issue that acts as the conduit between the stock exchanges and NPCI for UPI mandates.
SPVSpecial purpose vehicle.
Stabilising agentThe BRLM that manages post-listing price stabilisation under a Green Shoe Option.
Syndicate membersSEBI-registered intermediaries who collect bids and may underwrite.
Underwriting AgreementAgreement under which underwriters ensure payment or subscription for shares allocated to investors they procure.
UPIUnified Payments Interface, used by retail investors to block funds for an IPO bid.
VCFVenture Capital Fund registered with SEBI.
Wilful defaulterA borrower declared a wilful defaulter by a bank or financial institution under RBI guidelines.
